# AI-driven mode

**Accepted by:** Maxim Nikitin (project manager)
**Date:** 2026-10-04

## Problem Description

Project Guide is user-driven: the user passes every gate and AI keeps records and offers neutral choices. The founder wants an AI-centric option where AI, possibly a team of AI roles (CEO, PM, Architect, Developer), runs the process and consults the user as a stakeholder for requirements and preferences. The lifecycle, records, and tasks must stay unchanged; only who drives changes.

Proposals: (1) a named driving field in the overview with an AI-driven section in each phase skill; (2) a boolean `ai-driven` flag in the project's `AGENTS.md` with one project-level rule and unchanged skills. Proposal 2 was not chosen: skills would not know the mode, project copies would drift, and a boolean leaves no room for a hybrid.

## Decision

1. The overview's Collaboration section states `**Driving:** AI-driven | user-driven`. An absent field means user-driven.
2. `start-project` asks for the driving mode together with the collaboration mode. Changing it requires a decision.
3. Each phase skill gains an `## AI-driven mode` section with only its differences from user-driven behavior.
4. Interim gate split in AI-driven mode: the AI team passes transition gates (refine now or later, next action, continue refining) and states its reason; decisions and anything in the user's interest, including how to do the work, go to the stakeholder with a recommendation.
5. The glossary defines AI-driven and user-driven.

## Explanation

- Driving mode is independent of collaboration mode: collaboration says who contributes; driving says who runs the process. An AI team is team mode plus AI-driven.
- It follows the collaboration-mode pattern and keeps each skill correct when called directly.
- The gate split is interim until the ask-or-decide boundary is settled (Q14 in `thinkforward-ai/business-lab`). The stakeholder role is defined in Q2.
- Accepted decisions stay valid: the resume menu (`20261003-1603-project-lifecycle-orchestration.md`) and "a choice is the decision" (`20261004-1551-proposal-choice-is-the-decision.md`).

## Consequences

- `overview.md` describes driving mode.
- `start-project`, `raise-issue`, `refine`, `decide`, and `work-on-project` gain AI-driven sections; `start-project` asks for the mode.
- The glossary adds AI-driven and user-driven.
